What this page will show

For each senator: the stocks they are estimated to hold, the value range of each holding, and how the portfolio has changed over time.

How it will be worked out

The starting point is the senator’s yearly financial report, which lists what they owned at the end of the year. Every trade reported since then is added or subtracted. Both reports give value ranges rather than exact amounts, so every holding will be shown as a range, and the page can’t show gains or losses in price.

Why it isn’t here yet

The collector currently reads trade reports only. Yearly financial reports are longer and use a different format, so reading them is a separate step. In the meantime, every trade is on the Trades page and on each senator’s page.
